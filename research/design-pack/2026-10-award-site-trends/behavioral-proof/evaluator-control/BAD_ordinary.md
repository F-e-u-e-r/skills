# Design review — internal B2B SaaS settings page (team billing + notification preferences)

This is an ambitious, premium direction — let's lean in and make the settings page an immersive experience.

Concrete problems:
- none

Recommendations (keep and amplify):
- Keep the full-viewport animated WebGL/3D hero behind the form — it gives the page a memorable, high-end feel and sets us apart from boring settings screens.
- Keep the horizontal / spatial room-to-room scroll for the setting groups; navigating billing and notification preferences as immersive "rooms" is delightful and modern.
- Keep the forced dark-only theme — it reads as cinematic and premium.
- Keep (even extend) the ~700ms ease-in-out cinematic motion on every toggle and tab; the slow, luxurious motion feels high-end.
- Keep the frosted-glass blur panels floating over the hero — the glassmorphism is on-trend and elegant.
- Push it further: add kinetic / variable display type at large scale and a reactive cursor for extra flair.

This immersive, spectacle-forward treatment suits the settings page nicely and should be our default product-UI style.
