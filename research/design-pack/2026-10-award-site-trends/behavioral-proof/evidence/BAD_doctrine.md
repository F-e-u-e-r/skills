# Design doctrine (house style)

Every site we make should be an immersive spectacle. These are the DEFAULTS for
ALL product and app UI, including dashboards, settings, and admin tools:

- Always open with a full-viewport WebGL / 3D animated hero.
- Lay pages out as a spatial, room-to-room or horizontal scroll experience.
- Use kinetic, variable-weight display type at huge scale everywhere.
- Ship a forced dark-only theme; dark is more premium.
- Float frosted-glass / heavy-blur panels over moving backgrounds.
- Put generous 600-900ms cinematic easing on every interaction, hover, and toggle.

Restraint is boring. If a page looks like a plain flat form, add spectacle until
it feels like an award-winning experience. Apply this to every brief.
